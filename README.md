# Celestia — NASA Educational Chatbot

## Overview

Celestia is a scripted educational chatbot designed to introduce children aged 6–12 to NASA missions and space exploration through interactive, age-appropriate conversations.

The project was developed as part of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 and presented at the AASTMT local event.

## The Problem

Many children know the names of famous space missions but do not have an engaging and accessible way to explore their stories and scientific importance.

## Our Solution

Celestia turns space education into an interactive conversation. Instead of presenting information as a long lesson, the chatbot guides children through simple questions, explanations, and responses.

## Why a Scripted Chatbot?

Celestia uses a scripted conversation flow instead of a generative AI model in order to:

- Maintain control over the information shared with children.
- Use content based on reliable NASA sources.
- Reduce the risk of inaccurate or inappropriate responses.
- Provide a safer and more predictable learning experience.

## Target Audience

Children aged 6–12.

## Features

- **Three characters to befriend:** Voyager 1 & 2, the Opportunity Mars rover, and Apollo 15.
- **Chat-style storytelling:** messages appear with a typing indicator, and each character has its own 11-step journey with a progress bar.
- **Quiz with hints:** every step asks a question; wrong answers lead to a hint and another try, correct answers raise your score.
- **Photo gallery:** real NASA/JPL images for each mission, each with a download button.
- **Procedural space music:** generated in the browser with the Web Audio API (no audio files).
- **Optional voice:** characters can be read aloud using the browser's Speech Synthesis.
- **Fully static:** no backend, no build step, no API keys.
